# Archive

This folder contains completed, inactive, deprecated, or historical material retained for reference.

## What Belongs Here

- Completed or discontinued projects
- Superseded documentation and resources
- Historical records that should no longer appear as current work
- Deprecated procedures or prompts preserved for context

Treat archived information as historical until its current accuracy is verified.

## Project Status Distinctions

- **Demo-complete:** The approved demo gate is satisfied, but production readiness, operational acceptance, or future development may remain open.
- **Production-complete:** The approved production scope is deployed and verified, with handover and operational ownership recorded.
- **Cancelled:** Work ended by an explicit decision; completed evidence and the reason for cancellation remain preserved.
- **Deferred:** Work is intentionally postponed with a documented trigger or decision for reconsideration.
- **Archived:** The project passed the archive gate and its complete folder was moved here as historical material.

Demo-complete, production-complete, cancelled, and deferred describe project outcomes or current decisions. Archived describes the storage state after the archive procedure is completed.

## Project Archive Gate

A project may be archived only when:

- [ ] Its current phase is documented.
- [ ] Its final status is documented.
- [ ] Its next action is completed, cancelled, or intentionally deferred.
- [ ] Important documentation and evidence are preserved.
- [ ] Known limitations are documented.
- [ ] Pending Git changes are committed and pushed.
- [ ] The project owner explicitly approves archiving.

Do not treat an incomplete gate as permission to move the project.

## Safe Project Archive Procedure

1. Perform a final project review.
2. Confirm the project status, phase, owner, and production-readiness value.
3. Confirm the complete documentation set and important evidence are preserved.
4. Commit and push all approved pending project changes.
5. Move the complete project folder into `09 - Archive/Projects/`.
6. Update affected dashboard, hub, project, SOP, and related-note navigation links.
7. Confirm that no important wikilinks, attachments, records, or evidence were broken.

## Archive Safeguards

- Do not delete project evidence during archiving.
- Do not archive a project simply because development paused.
- A demo-complete project may remain in `02 - Projects` when future work is still possible.
- Production projects require completed handover and documented operational ownership before archiving.
- Preserve inactive, cancelled, and deferred decisions with their rationale and evidence.
- Do not treat archived information as current without verification.

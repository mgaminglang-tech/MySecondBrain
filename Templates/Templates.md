# Templates

This folder contains reusable Obsidian templates for creating consistently structured notes.

## What Belongs Here

- Project, knowledge, SOP, AI prompt, daily, and meeting templates
- Obsidian template placeholders such as `{{date:YYYY-MM-DD}}`
- Generic fields and guidance that can be reused without project-specific data

Preserve template placeholders and avoid adding real credentials or sensitive information. Duplicate client-automation templates into the relevant project folder before use; update the project copy and keep the reusable source template unchanged.

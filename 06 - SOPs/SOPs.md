# Standard Operating Procedures

This folder contains repeatable procedures for completing operational and technical tasks consistently.

## What Belongs Here

- Procedures with clear prerequisites and safety considerations
- Numbered steps with expected results
- Verification and rollback instructions
- Troubleshooting guidance and links to related notes

Only describe an SOP as tested when evidence is available.
